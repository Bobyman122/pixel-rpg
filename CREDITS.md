# Credits

Crystal Quest's code and story are original. The art, music and sound effects come from two free packs, and both artists deserve the thanks.

## Ninja Adventure Asset Pack

By **Pixel-boy** and **AAA** · https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Everything except the battle monsters: map tiles, every character and portrait, the Giant Blue Samurai boss, menus and windows, battle effects, item icons, music, jingles and sound effects.

Released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Credit isn't required, but it's well earned. The licence text is in `public/assets/LICENSE-ninja-adventure.txt`.

## Free Mythic Monsters

By **Willibab** · https://willibab.itch.io/free-mythic-monsters

The battle monsters: the slime, goblin, sabrecat, hornet, cave spider and skeleton.

Released under **Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)**. You can use, share and change them, including commercially, as long as Willibab is credited. If you fork this project, keep this credit (it is also shown in the game's ending credits).

## What was changed

- Music was re-encoded to MP3 with one fixed volume adjustment per track, so they all play at a similar loudness.
- Sound effects were converted to 22 kHz mono WAV.
- A few equipment icons had their coloured background tiles removed so they sit cleanly in the menus.
- Sprites were picked from the packs and renamed. They weren't otherwise edited.
